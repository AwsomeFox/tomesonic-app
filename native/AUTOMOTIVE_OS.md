# Android Automotive OS app — exploration & implementation plan

Goal: TomeSonic in cars that run **Android Automotive OS** (AAOS — Polestar, Volvo,
GM, Renault, Honda; the OS *in* the car, no phone involved): browse the library,
resume the current book, stream or play downloads, sign in on the car screen, with
progress syncing through the ABS server like every other client.

This file is the plan and the why. The **implementation contract** is
`native/automotive/ARCHITECTURE.md` — where it and this plan disagree, the contract
wins. The **release runbook** (Play form factor, automotive screenshots, the stricter
car review, how three AABs ship together) will be `PLAY_STORE_AUTOMOTIVE.md` at the
repo root (Wave 5).

## What already works today — and what AAOS is not

**Android Auto (projection)** already works: the patched RNTP `MusicService`
(`patches/react-native-track-player+5.0.0-alpha0.patch`, class
`com.doublesymmetry.trackplayer.service.MusicService`, ~3.5k lines) is a
`MediaLibraryService` with a complete ABS browse tree, offline downloads view,
progress badges, search, and cold-start playback. A phone running TomeSonic projects
all of that onto the head unit.

**AAOS is a different thing**: the app runs *on the car*, with no phone. Nothing we
ship reaches those cars today. The car's own Media Center renders browse and
now-playing around a `MediaLibraryService` — so an AAOS media app is "that same
service, running in the car", plus on-car sign-in. (`native/ANDROID_AUTO.md` predates
the shipped Auto implementation and describes it as TODO — trust the code, not that
doc's status section.)

## The non-negotiable: a separate native module, mandated by Play

Two reasons, one of them Google's rule:

1. **Play requires it.** Media apps distribute to AAOS on a dedicated
   `automotive:` form-factor track, and a media artifact cannot serve both mobile
   and Automotive — the AAOS AAB is its own artifact by policy, not by preference.
2. **The RN entanglement.** The patched `MusicService` lives in `node_modules`
   (regenerated by `npm ci` + patch-package) and branches on `reactContext != null`
   on every play path. It cannot be depended on as a library, and re-splitting a
   3.5k-line patched file on every RNTP bump is not a maintenance plan.

So: **wear-style independent port into `native/automotive/`** — a plain Kotlin
Android application module, no RN, no Compose UI beyond a sign-in form and a
settings screen (the car draws everything else). Port the data / playback /
downloads layers from `native/wear/` (already RN-free, coroutine-based, and
JVM-tested) and the **browse tree** from the patch (the one thing wear doesn't
have). The accepted cost — a third copy of the ABS read path — is the same trade
wear made explicitly; once wear and automotive both hold RN-free copies, extracting
a shared `:abs-core` from *those two* is a later, safe refactor that never touches
the patch.

## Where the code lives (the prebuild wrinkle, again)

`expo prebuild --clean` deletes and regenerates `native/android`, so the module
lives outside it: **`native/automotive/`** (sibling of `android/` and `wear/`).
`include ':automotive'` lines are committed in `android/settings.gradle` AND
re-injected by `plugins/withAutomotiveApp.js` (marker-guarded, template-shape
asserted), exactly like `withWearApp.js`.

## Auth: there is no phone to mirror from

Wear v1 leaned on phone-mirrored credentials; a car cannot. AAOS auth is the wear
**v2 watch-login path, promoted to primary** — plus two car-specific requirements:

- **Sign-in Activity**: a normal full-screen form (cars have touch keyboards — no
  RemoteInput dance), reachable from the Media Center via the media session's
  auth-error resolution intent. Parked-only by system policy; it must NOT carry
  `distractionOptimized` (instant review rejection).
- **AccountManager**: an `AbstractAccountAuthenticator` registration is documented
  as mandatory for car media apps (account management from car settings, guest-mode
  `DISALLOW_MODIFY_ACCOUNTS` behavior). Net-new work — wear has no equivalent.
- The token machinery ports from wear verbatim: `POST /login`,
  single-flight `POST /auth/refresh` (`x-refresh-token`, empty body,
  rotation-optional), and the `RefreshPolicy` decision table where only 401/403
  from `/auth/refresh` ends the session.

## Browse

Same tree, same endpoints, fourth client — ported from the patch with the RN
branches deleted: `__ROOT__` → Continue Listening, Continue Series, Downloads,
Libraries; per-library categories; authors/series/collections; progress badges and
"42% • Title" prefixes; offline → Downloads-only tree. The grammar, badge extras,
caching discipline (45 s fresh / 10 min stale-on-failure), Binder art budget, and
the INTERNET+VALIDATED network gate are all frozen in the contract. Search ports
too — it is how Assistant/Gemini voice commands reach the app (a review
requirement, not a nicety).

## Playback & downloads

Wear's `playback/` and `downloads/` packages are the donors: `SessionManager`
(own deviceInfo, `clientName: "TomeSonic Automotive"`), `ProgressSyncer` +
`OfflineProgressQueue` (wall-clock `timeListened`, two-queue offline flush,
`automotive-local_` session-id prefix so no client can replace another's stats),
Media3 with the authorized DataSource feeding both audio and cover art, and the
WorkManager download worker — cars have Wi-Fi when parked and big screens for
managing storage.

## Release plumbing

- Same `applicationId` (`com.tomesonic.app`), third AAB. versionCode =
  **phone + 2,000,000** (phone 20154 → wear 1020154 → automotive 2020154), derived
  at configure time from `android/app/build.gradle` exactly as wear does.
- `deploy-playstore.yml` gains a third upload step, `automotive:${track}`, ordered
  phone → wear → automotive so a missing car track can never block shipped
  artifacts; the "verify AABs exist" guard extends to three files.
- Play Console: opt in to the **Android Automotive OS form factor**. Car review is
  **stricter than wear**: blocking on open testing/production (and, with a shared
  package, a car rejection can hold a bundled phone submission — so a first AAOS
  submission always rides its own release). Closed testing gets a *non-blocking*
  written review — the free dress rehearsal. Review needs working test-server
  credentials in the Console (self-hosted app — a reachable demo ABS instance is a
  release-checklist item).

## Dev & testing

The AAOS emulator (`android-automotive` images, API 34/35) covers everything but
real-car audio focus: browse via the car Media Center, sign-in, VHAL-simulated
driving/parked states for the distraction rules. CI screenshots ride the wear rig
pattern (`reactivecircus/android-emulator-runner` supports `target:
android-automotive`), with one twist: there is **no launcher activity** — the rig
opens the app through the Media Center intent, not `am start`. The two bundled
emulator profiles (1024p landscape, portrait) are exactly Play's two screenshot
floors.
